NUHA TikTok Automation

NUHA TikTok Automation is a Windows desktop application designed to help users manage uploads of their own videos and captions to TikTok through the official Content Posting API.

How it works

Users select their own video files, review their captions, and authorize access to their TikTok account through TikTok's official authorization process. Videos are transferred using the permissions approved for the application.

Users remain responsible for reviewing and completing the posting workflow in TikTok. The application does not automatically attach affiliate products.
