Privacy Policy

NUHA TikTok Automation

Effective date: October 19, 2026

1. Information We Process

The application may process video files, captions, and TikTok account authorization information that users provide to use its features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the requested video upload functionality, maintain application security, and troubleshoot errors.

3. TikTok Authorization

When users authorize the application through TikTok, access is limited to the permissions granted by TikTok. Users should not share their access tokens or credentials.

4. Storage and Security

We aim to protect information using reasonable security measures. The actual information stored depends on the application configuration. Sensitive authorization tokens must be stored securely and must not be published in source code.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

Information is not sold. Information may be transmitted to TikTok as necessary to provide user-requested functionality or disclosed when legally required.

6. Retention and Deletion

Users should be able to remove locally stored application data and revoke the application's access through TikTok settings. Contact the application operator for questions about deletion.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the application develops. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the contact method provided with the application.
